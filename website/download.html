Barnaby 0.1.0, an early version

For Windows 10 (version 22H2) and Windows 11.

File name: Barnaby-Setup-0.1.0.exe

Download Barnaby for Windows

Only download Barnaby from hellobarnaby.com. If an email, text or caller sends you a link to “Barnaby”, don't click it. We never send links like that.

What you need

Windows 10 or Windows 11

Windows 10 needs version 22H2. To check: press the Windows key, type winver, and press Enter. A box shows the version.

The internet

Barnaby needs the internet to understand you and to help. Wi-Fi or a cable, either is fine.

A microphone and speakers

Most laptops have both built in. No microphone? You can type to Barnaby instead.

A little free space

Less than 1 GB. Most computers have plenty.

Barnaby doesn't work on a Mac, iPad, Chromebook or phone yet. Computers in “S mode” only install apps from the Microsoft Store, so Barnaby can't be installed on them yet.

Installing, step by step

  1. Click “Download Barnaby for Windows”

    The file saves onto the computer, in the Downloads folder. In Microsoft Edge, a small box appears at the top right.

  2. If Edge says the file “isn't commonly downloaded”

    Edge says this about new programs. Click the three dots (…) next to the file name, then “Keep”. If it asks again, click “Show more”, then “Keep anyway”.

  3. Open the file

    Click “Open file” under the file name. Or open the Downloads folder, click Barnaby-Setup-0.1.0 once, and press Enter.

  4. If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”

    Click “More info”, the underlined words. Then click “Run anyway”.

    Why does Windows warn about Barnaby?

    Windows warns about programs it hasn't seen many times. Barnaby is new, and this early version doesn't carry a Microsoft-recognised signature yet. That's why it says “Unknown publisher”.

    Nothing is wrong with your computer, or with Barnaby. We're working on removing this step.

    Only click “Run anyway” for the Barnaby file you downloaded yourself, from hellobarnaby.com. If this blue box appears for something a caller, email or pop-up told you to download, click “Don't run”.

  5. Barnaby installs by itself

    There's nothing to choose. After a moment Barnaby opens, and a “Barnaby” picture appears on the desktop.

  6. Answer the setup questions

    Barnaby asks a few questions, one at a time: what to call you, the text size, your email, and a few more. See every question.

Removing Barnaby

Changed your mind? Open Start, then Settings, then Apps. Find Barnaby in the list and choose Uninstall.

To remove what Barnaby knows first, use “Delete everything Barnaby knows” in his settings. What Barnaby keeps.

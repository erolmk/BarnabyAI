What Barnaby looks like

What Barnaby does

A helper who does it with you

Email, photos, video calls, the internet, and the computer itself. One helper, in plain words.

Talk to him

Press “Talk to Barnaby”, press the F9 key, or just say “Hello, Barnaby” (if you turn that on). Then say what you'd like. Use your own words. There are no commands to learn. Rather type? That works too.

You might say“Can you help me see the photos Tom sent?”

He does it with you, and shows you how

Before each click, Barnaby rings the spot and says what it does. You do the personal parts: choosing, typing passwords, pressing Send. Each task becomes a lesson you keep, in big print, ready to print.

Barnaby says“I'm clicking the blue ‘New mail’ button, top left. That starts a new email.”

Scam Shield

Barnaby reads the words on your screen, on your computer, to spot tricks like fake virus warnings. If something looks wrong, he says so calmly and offers to close it.

Barnaby says“This page is pretending to be Microsoft. It's a trick. Your computer is fine.”

Fixes a slow computer

Say “my computer is slow”, or “no sound”, or “the internet stopped”. Barnaby looks first and changes nothing. He explains what he found, and fixes things only after you say yes.

Barnaby says“Your computer isn't broken. It's busy. 14 programs start by themselves every morning.”

Peace of mind for family

If your parent says yes, you get a message on your phone when Barnaby warns about a scam. Then you can call. Family can't see the screen, emails or websites.

Example message“Rose's computer helper showed a scam warning: fake virus pop-up. A kind call from you would help.”

Safety promises

You stay in charge

Barnaby can see and use the computer. So we built him around three promises that no web page, email or caller can change.

He never presses Send for you

Before anything is sent, bought or deleted, Barnaby shows exactly what will happen, in big print. Then you press the last button yourself.

He never lets a stranger take over your computer

Programs that let someone control your computer from far away are blocked. Fake “tech support” callers rely on them.

He never asks for money

Barnaby won't help pay with gift cards, Bitcoin or wire transfers. No real company, bank or government asks for those.

Barnaby will never phone you, never ask for money, and never ask for gift cards.

If someone calls and says they're Barnaby, hang up. It's a scam. Read all our safety and privacy promises.

Questions

Questions families ask

Is it safe?

Barnaby is built so that you stay in charge. You press Send, Buy and Delete yourself. He refuses to open programs that let strangers control your computer, and won't help pay with gift cards or Bitcoin.

Scam Shield watches for fake warnings while you use the computer. Read the safety promises.

What does Barnaby see?

While helping with something you asked for, he looks at your screen, so he knows where to click. A small eye picture shows when he's looking. Passwords and card numbers are blacked out first.

When the task ends, he stops looking.

What about privacy?

Your lessons, and the things Barnaby remembers, stay on your computer. Screen pictures and voice recordings are never saved. There are no ads, and we never sell your information.

See exactly what leaves the computer.

What if I don't have Windows 11?

Barnaby works on Windows 10 (version 22H2) and Windows 11. Here's how to check which one you have.

Barnaby doesn't work on a Mac, iPad, Chromebook or phone yet.

What does it cost?

The Free plan costs nothing and includes Scam Shield. The Family plan is $9.99 a month, with a 30-day free trial. You can cancel with one click.

Can I use it without a family member?

Yes. Barnaby is made to help you on your own. He guides you through setup, one question at a time. Family alerts are optional, and only happen if you say yes.

Does it work with Outlook, Gmail, AOL, Yahoo and iCloud?

Yes. Barnaby works with Outlook (the website or the program), Gmail, AOL Mail and Yahoo Mail. With iCloud, he helps with your iPhone photos and iCloud Mail, on the iCloud website.

He also helps with Google Photos, Zoom, WhatsApp and Facebook.

Is Barnaby a real person?

No. Barnaby is a computer program that understands everyday speech. Nobody is watching your screen or listening in.

And Barnaby will never phone you.
